The site ships with a built-in TMDB key, so it works out of the box. You can optionally paste your own free key (themoviedb.org → Settings → API) — it's stored only in your browser and takes precedence.

Backup & Transfer

Export your profiles, watch history, manga progress, and settings to a file — or import a previously exported file to restore them (replaces current data).

Online Sync (experimental)

Link devices into a sync room so profiles and continue-watching/reading progress are shared between them — e.g. your friend's profile appears on your device and yours on theirs. Profiles merge by name; data is end-to-end encrypted with the room passphrase and stored only as ciphertext.
